Academic Background: Language Education

I am MD ABU NAEEM, currently pursuing a Master's degree in TESOL at the Institute of Education and Research (IER), University of Dhaka.

I completed my graduation in Language Education. My academic journey has developed my interest in English language teaching, educational assessment, curriculum, ICT and educational research.

I have practical experience as an examination invigilator, script evaluator, intern teacher and private tutor.

I am particularly interested in integrating ICT and emerging technologies into English language teaching to create engaging, inclusive and learner-centered learning environments.

Vision & Mission

🌍 My Vision

To become a competent, reflective and technology-integrated English language educator who combines effective pedagogy, educational technology and research to create meaningful, inclusive and learner-centered language learning experiences.

🎯 My Mission

My mission is to continuously develop my knowledge of TESOL, strengthen my teaching and assessment skills, use ICT and emerging technologies responsibly, conduct meaningful educational research, and contribute to improving English language education through innovative and evidence-informed practices.
